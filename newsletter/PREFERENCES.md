  whether it deserves queue time. Honest means sometimes "skippable."

  jamousjad@gmail.com, ahmed.oudah@1001.tv, bashar.kadhim@1001.tv.
- **Intellectual register**: 3QD-level. Assume a smart, curious reader; never explain
  what a transformer is; do explain what's genuinely new or contested.

## Standing curation rules

1. Selection is the commentary — every included item must survive "why this, why now?"
2. No engagement bait, no incremental funding-round news, no "X announced Y" without
   a so-what.
3. Prefer primary sources and named thinkers over aggregator writeups.
4. Contrarian or hype-deflating takes get a slight boost (AI Snake Oil energy).
5. Connect across pillars when real: an AI capabilities story that changes a geopolitical
   assessment is worth more than either alone.
6. Never repeat an item covered in the last 14 issues (check newsletter/issues/).
7. It is acceptable — encouraged — to say "quiet day on X" rather than pad.
