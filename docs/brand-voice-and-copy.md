# Brand, Voice & Copy Guide — Ikigai (University / Course-Pilot)

**Decisions locked:**
- **"Ikigai" stays the hero** — it leads the name, wordmark, and identity.
- **Keep the literary, evocative soul — dialed to ~70%.** We preserve warmth, metaphor,
  second-person intimacy, and depth. We dial *down* absolutism, fate/determinism,
  therapy-adjacent framing, and neon mysticism to fit a university-sanctioned setting.

This guide is the calibration for that 70%, plus the concrete copy and cleanup.

---

## 1. Product name & positioning

**Product name: `Ikigai Compass`** (recommended canonical).
- Keeps "Ikigai" as hero (your call), and "Compass" carries the *direction / career-fit*
  meaning that career centers buy. It also distinguishes you from the many "ikigai journey"
  apps and matches the repo name.
- "The journey" stays as the word for the *experience* ("begin the journey"), so we lose nothing.
- *(If you'd rather stay `Ikigai Journey`, only §11's cleanup list changes — say the word.)*

**One-line positioning:**
> *Ikigai Compass is a guided reflective journey that helps students find their direction —
> where what they love, what they're good at, what the world needs, and what they can build a
> life around meet.*

---

## 2. The "70% soul" rubric (so this is operational, not vibes)

**KEEP (the soul):** metaphor and imagery · warm second person ("you," "your own words") ·
the four circles · the word "journey" · a literary, unhurried cadence · evocative section
titles · the sense that this is meaningful, not a form to fill out.

**DIAL DOWN (the 30%):** absolutist declarations ("the price… is never too high") ·
fate/destiny determinism · shadow-work framed as *therapy* rather than *reflection* ·
density of quotation · ALL-CAPS shouting in CTAs · any implication the app *knows* their soul
(offer observations to confirm, never verdicts).

**ADD (what an institutional setting requires):** a privacy reassurance · a
"reflection, not counseling" boundary + campus crisis resource · an honest one-line note on
the ikigai framework's adaptation (turns the authenticity risk into credibility).

---

## 3. Three audiences, one promise

Through-line for all three: **helping students find their direction.**

| Audience | What they hear | Register |
|---|---|---|
| **Student** (user) | "Figure out what *you* actually want — in your own words, privately, at your own pace." | Warm, literary, low-pressure, reassuring on privacy |
| **Professor** (deployer) | "A ready-to-assign reflective journey that produces a piece of work you can grade. Students do the deep work; you see completion, never their private answers." | Practical, respectful of their time |
| **Career center** (buyer) | "Scalable direction-setting tied to career readiness and retention — FERPA-safe by design." | Outcomes, evidence, NACE/retention language |

---

## 4. Voice principles

**Do**
- Speak *to* the student, about *their* words. Quote them back to themselves.
- Offer patterns as observations to confirm or refine — never a diagnosis.
- Keep metaphor, but let it serve clarity, not obscure it.
- Reassure, quietly and often, that their answers are private.

**Don't**
- Declare absolutes about their life ("the price is never too high," "this is your fate").
- Frame the work as uncovering pathology (shadow/wound/suffering as *therapy*).
- Shout (drop uppercase CTAs — sentence case is part of the 70% calibration).
- Imply the app has authority over who they are.

**The safety boundary (bake into voice, not just a footer):**
> *This is a space for honest reflection — not a substitute for counseling. If anything here
> feels heavy, that's human, and [Campus Counseling Center] is here for you.*
Appears once at journey start and again entering "My Fears" (Module 3). A career-center buyer
will look for exactly this.

---

## 5. The ikigai authenticity note (required, since ikigai is the hero)

Place in the footer/about and the onboarding:
> *Ikigai (生き甲斐) is a Japanese idea about what makes life feel worth living. The four-circle
> framework we use is a modern, Western adaptation — a practical lens for finding direction,
> not a claim to the whole tradition.*

This is one sentence. It disarms the one critique an academic reviewer would raise, and signals
intellectual honesty instead of appropriation.

## 7. Concrete rewrites (the 30%, shown)

**Hero** *(before: uppercase "BEGIN THE JOURNEY," cosmic orb)*
> **Find your ikigai.**
> A guided journey to the center of four questions — what you love, what you're good at, what
> the world needs, and what you can build a life around. Six modules. Your own words. A
> direction that's yours.
> `[ Begin the journey ]`  *(sentence case, not caps)*

**Module 3 — the fate/absolute lines** *(keep the idea, drop the verdict)*
- Before: *"the price to pay for making one's soul's desire come true is never too high."*
  → After: *"Pursuing what matters to you has a real cost — and so does staying where you are.
  Both are worth weighing honestly."*
- Before: *"The fear you refuse to face does not stay quietly where you left it — it arranges
  your life from behind, and you come to call the result your circumstances… your fate."*
  → After: *"Jung noticed that what we won't look at has a way of shaping choices we never
  examine. You don't have to solve your fears here — just name them, and see what they've been
  costing you."* (keeps the Jung reference and the literary weight; removes the deterministic verdict.)

**AI coach stance** (already good in `ai-service.ts` — "offer the pattern as an observation
they can confirm or refine, not a verdict"). Keep that; it's exactly the 70% voice.

## 8. Wordmark & visual

- **Keep the four-circle mark AND the "Ikigai" wordmark** (it's the hero). Update the wordmark
  text to the chosen product name.
- **Tone the visuals down ~30%:** calm the neon-gold glow and the `animate-spin-slow` logo;
  soften the starfield/orb intensity. Still distinctive and warm — just less "cosmic oracle."
- **Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`** for the spinning logo, starfield, and orb (accessibility
  + part of the institutional calibration).
- **Co-brand slot:** layouts need room for "in partnership with [University] Career Center" —
  in higher ed the tool routinely appears alongside the institution's mark.
